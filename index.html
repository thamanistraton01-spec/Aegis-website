Fraud Detection

Automated flags for synthetic P&Ls, shell accounts and more.

ATLAS examines up to three years of revenue data, calculating year-on-year growth rates and identifying volatility patterns that indicate distressed or manipulated financials. Thin-file businesses receive a conservative risk premium — protecting lenders from underdocumented applicants without excluding them entirely.

Every SME is stress-tested against a debt-to-revenue ratio threshold. Businesses exceeding 1.2x leverage receive an automatic Toxic Leverage flag — surfaced directly on the dashboard before any loan officer reviews the file. This alone can prevent your most damaging NPLs.

Where M-Pesa statements are available, ATLAS measures cash velocity — the longest gap between income credits, median account runway, and daily burn rate. Sector-aware multipliers adjust thresholds for Agriculture (seasonal) vs Retail (daily cash flow). Shell and dormant accounts are automatically flagged as AML risk.

ATLAS runs seven proprietary heuristics including synthetic P&L detection, ghost company checks, and volatile cashflow traps. Each flag is labelled and exportable — giving your credit committee a documented, auditable reason for every decline.
